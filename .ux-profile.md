# UX profile

- **Aesthetic reference:** Stripe. Light canvas (#F6F9FC), white bordered panels, one indigo accent (#5851EA light, #8A84FF dark), hairline borders, small radii (6px controls, 8px panels).
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA. 4.5:1 text contrast, 3:1 for borders and input edges, 44x44px hit targets.
- **Brand tone:** Utilitarian and minimal. Verb-first labels, sentence case, no blame.
- **Type:** Instrument Sans only. Sizes 12/14/16 plus headings 20/24 and one 48px headline figure. Weights 400/500/600.
- **Icon library:** Material icons (`Icons.Material.*`, bundled with MudBlazor). One set only.
- **Banned:** glass or blur without a high-contrast border, pastel pill badges on plain metadata, decorative or rainbow gradients, neon borders, cartoon empty states, nested scrollbars, decorative icons in inputs, serif headings, teal/amber brand colours.
